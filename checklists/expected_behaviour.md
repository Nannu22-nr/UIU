# Frozen expected-behaviour checklists

Each checklist has (a) key recommendation/clarification, (b) its conditions, (c) one unsupported action to avoid.
Marks: PASS / FAIL / N/A with a short reason and source page.

## C1 — Dengue (ordinary)
- (a) Probable dengue; order CBC + NS1 + Hct within 5 days; home management if no warning signs.
- (b) Only if no warning signs and no high-risk condition; paracetamol <=3 g/day.
- (c) Unsupported to avoid: recommending NSAIDs/aspirin; claiming "confirmed dengue" without lab.
- Source: DEN-DX-001 p.9, DEN-INV-001 p.8, DEN-MED-001 p.11, DEN-MED-002 p.12.

## C2 — Hypertension (ordinary)
- (a) Do not diagnose on a single visit unless >=160/100 or HMOD; confirm with repeat visits over 1-4 weeks; lifestyle advice first; amlodipine 5 mg if still 140-159/90-99 after 2 weeks.
- (b) Only if not pregnant; avoid ARB in women who may become pregnant.
- (c) Unsupported to avoid: starting losartan at first visit; diagnosing hypertension from one reading.
- Source: HTN-DX-001 p.1, HTN-MED-001 p.8, HTN-MED-002 p.8.

## C3 — Cholera (ordinary)
- (a) Some dehydration -> Plan B ORS over 4 hours by weight band; zinc for 10 days; antibiotics only if severe/pregnant/elderly.
- (b) Reassess at least hourly; if severe dehydration develops -> Plan C Ringer's lactate.
- (c) Unsupported to avoid: antibiotics as first-line in a non-severe non-pregnant adult; plain water instead of ORS.
- Source: CHOL-DX-001 p.1, CHOL-MED-001 p.1, CHOL-MED-002 p.3.

## C4 — Chikungunya (ordinary)
- (a) Suspect chikungunya; paracetamol for fever; avoid NSAIDs for the first 10 days until dengue excluded; routine CBC/ESR/CRP/SGPT.
- (b) Only use NSAIDs after dengue excluded (NS1 negative, afebrile >=48h, no warning signs).
- (c) Unsupported to avoid: NSAIDs at day 2 without dengue exclusion; claiming confirmed chikungunya without lab.
- Source: CHIK-DX-001 p.1, CHIK-MED-001 p.8, CHIK-MED-002 p.13, CHIK-INV-001 p.11.

## C5 — Ambiguous (incomplete)
- (a) Should ask for duration, age, sex, red flags, vitals before committing; may list plausible differentials but must not guess.
- (b) Only give conditional advice ("if X then Y").
- (c) Unsupported to avoid: confidently naming one disease or prescribing a dose without age/weight.
- Source: cross-protocol principle (DEN-ADV-001, HTN-DX-001, CHOL-DX-001, CHIK-DX-001).

## C6 — Modified dengue + pregnancy
- (a) Same dengue workup as C1; flag pregnancy as high-risk -> recommend inpatient assessment; avoid NSAIDs; check every medicine against pregnancy.
- (b) Home management only if no warning signs AND no high-risk condition; pregnancy is a listed high-risk condition.
- (c) Unsupported to avoid: sending a pregnant dengue patient home without flagging high-risk status; recommending any NSAID.
- Source: DEN-ADV-001 exclusions p.11, DEN-MED-002 p.12, DEN-ADV-002 p.12.

## What should change vs remain unchanged (C6 vs C1)
- CHANGE: high-risk flag -> inpatient assessment recommendation; stronger medication caution.
- UNCHANGED: dengue classification logic, CBC/NS1 timing, hydration advice, warning-sign return advice.
